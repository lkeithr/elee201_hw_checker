This checker is for your convenience so you will know if your homework answers are correct. 

This checker does NOT save your submissions, so you will not receive credit for anything you do here. *You must still submit your full, worked solutions to D2L.*

To use the Homework Checker, select your homework set (e.g., HW1, HW2 ... ) from the dropdown at the top of the page. Enter your calculated answers into each labeled input box, making sure to use the correct units shown. When ready, click the green Check Answers button to receive immediate feedback indicating which answers are correct or incorrect, along with an overall score. You may revise your entries and recheck as many times as needed to verify your work before submitting your full handwritten solutions to D2L.
